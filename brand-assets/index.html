Brand Assets

The FOIS logo, colour palette and typefaces, for anyone referencing us in coverage, decks or partner materials.

Download Logo (PNG)

Usage Guidelines

Using the FOIS logo

Please keep the logo intact — don't stretch, distort, recolour or add effects to it.

Give it clear space on all sides, roughly equal to the height of the mark, so it doesn't crowd other text or imagery.

Avoid placing it on busy photography or backgrounds that reduce its legibility. On dark backgrounds, the light version above should read clearly; if it doesn't, choose a plainer background.

Typography

Two typefaces

Cormorant Garamond — a serif used for all headings and display type, set light and often paired with an italic gold accent word.

Inter — a sans-serif used for body copy, navigation, buttons and all UI text.

FOIS TV

Conversations, mini-documentaries and highlights from the platform. FOIS TV is the moving-image home of the initiative — where the people, projects and ideas behind the built environment are seen and heard, not just read about.

Follow FOIS TV

Show Formats

Built around three formats

FOIS TV is being developed around a small set of recurring formats, each suited to a different kind of story — the quick studio exchange, the slower observed documentary, and the community moment worth capturing as it happens.

Studio Conversations
Sit-down interviews and panel discussions with designers, architects and industry leaders, recorded in a dedicated studio setting.
Mini-Documentaries
Short-form films following a single project or space from brief to completion, told through the people who made it.
Campus & Community Highlights
Behind-the-scenes footage from Maven School, FOIS events and the wider community — critiques, exhibitions, and student work in progress.

Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Last updated: September 2026

1. Our commitment

Future of Interior Spaces is committed to making this website usable by as wide a range of people as possible, including people with disabilities. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA as our ongoing goal for the Site.

2. Where we are today

This is a new and evolving site. We have not yet carried out a formal, independent accessibility audit, and we expect to find — and fix — issues as we go. Accessibility is something we are actively working toward, not something we are claiming to have already fully achieved.

3. Known limitation: the custom cursor

This site uses a custom, circular cursor-follow effect as part of its visual design. We are aware that an effect like this may not suit everyone — for example, people using screen magnification, certain assistive technologies, or people who simply find it distracting. Standard browser cursor and keyboard focus behavior continues to operate underneath this effect: it is a decorative visual layer, not a replacement for normal browser interaction. We have not formally tested this effect with assistive technology, and we are keeping it in mind as an area for ongoing review and possible adjustment.

4. Keyboard and screen-reader use

We intend for the Site to be navigable using a keyboard and usable with screen readers. If you find a page, link, or piece of content that is not accessible to you in this way, please let us know using the details below — this kind of feedback directly helps us prioritize fixes.

5. Reporting an accessibility issue

If you experience any difficulty accessing content on this Site, please email hello@futureofinteriorspaces.com describing the issue, the page it occurred on, and, if possible, the browser and any assistive technology you were using. We will do our best to respond and to address the issue.

6. Ongoing effort

This statement will be reviewed and updated as the Site develops and as we make accessibility improvements. We see this as a continuing commitment rather than a one-time fix.

This Accessibility Statement is issued by Maven School of Interior Decoration and Design, operator of Future of Interior Spaces.

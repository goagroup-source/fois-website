Why FOIS Exists

No Single Profession Will Design Tomorrow Alone

The spaces we create shape the lives we live. Yet the industries responsible for shaping those spaces continue to work apart. Interior designers rarely sit with technology innovators. Architects often work separately from manufacturers. Property developers and design educators do not always share the same conversations.

This fragmentation has a cost. The most important problems facing the built environment — how we live, work, learn, heal and connect — cross every one of these disciplines at once. Solving them requires a shared table, not six separate ones.

For more than fifteen years, Maven School of Interior Decoration and Design has educated the designers who shape these spaces. But Maven came to believe that education alone is no longer enough. The industry needed a permanent, independent platform — one built not around a classroom, but around the entire global community responsible for the future of interior spaces.

That platform is Future of Interior Spaces (FOIS). We believe every great space begins with a great conversation — and that the future will be experienced through the spaces we design together.

We believe the future of interior spaces will not be designed by one profession alone.

Our Mission

Bringing the Built Environment Into One Conversation

FOIS exists to connect the people, ideas and disciplines shaping the future of interior spaces — through research, dialogue, storytelling and an annual gathering that brings the platform's community together in person.

We publish the research, host the conversations, and build the community that carries this thinking forward every week of the year — as a global initiative of Maven School of Interior Decoration and Design, existing for the whole industry.

The Platform

Six Pillars Shaping the Future of Space

FOIS is built around six content pillars that represent the forces transforming interior spaces across the world.

◆

Design

Interior design, spatial planning, colour theory, material innovation and the art of creating environments that elevate human experience.

◈

Architecture

The intersection of architecture and interiors, adaptive reuse, heritage restoration, and the future of building design.

◉

Property

Real estate development, urban planning, workspace strategy, residential trends and the economics of space.

◎

Technology

Smart homes, AI-driven design, digital fabrication, immersive environments and the technology reshaping how we create.

◐

Sustainability

Circular design, biophilic principles, sustainable materials, carbon-neutral interiors and regenerative architecture.

◑

Innovation

Emerging business models, new manufacturing methods, cross-disciplinary collaboration and the future of the design industry.

A Global Initiative by Maven School of Interior Decoration and Design

Born From a Teaching Institution

For more than fifteen years, Maven School of Interior Decoration and Design has educated designers, nurtured creativity and championed professional excellence.

As the design industry evolves through technology, sustainability and changing ways of living and working, Maven recognised the need for something bigger than education alone.

Future of Interior Spaces (FOIS) was created as a global initiative to bring together designers, architects, developers, manufacturers, educators, technology companies, policymakers and innovators to shape the future of interior spaces.

While FOIS is an initiative of Maven, it exists for the entire industry.

Every conversation. Every collaboration. Every innovation. Every future space. Begins here.

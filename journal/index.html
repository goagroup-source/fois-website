FOIS Journal

Editorial, interviews and thought leadership for the built environment. The Journal is where FOIS gathers the ideas, practitioners and provocations shaping how interior spaces are designed, built and lived in.

What You'll Find Here

Four strands of editorial

The Journal is structured around a small number of recurring editorial strands, each built to reward a different kind of reader — the practitioner scanning for ideas on a lunch break, and the student reading closely for craft.

Interviews
Long-form conversations with designers, architects, educators and manufacturers on how they think, decide and work — not just what they've made.
Opinion & Thought Leadership
Argued positions on where the industry is heading — materials, technology, sustainability, business models — written by practitioners with a point of view.
Project Features
Considered walkthroughs of completed spaces, told through process and decision-making rather than photography alone.
Industry News
Concise coverage of what's moving across the built environment — appointments, launches, research and the conversations that follow.

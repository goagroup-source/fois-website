Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. The short answer

This site does not currently use cookies for tracking, advertising, or analytics purposes. We do not run Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or any similar tool, and no tracking cookies of any kind are set when you browse this site.

2. What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file that a website can store on your device to remember information about your visit — for example, your preferences, or how you interact with the site. Cookies can be used for something as simple as remembering that you're logged in, or as involved as tracking your activity across many different websites for advertising purposes.

3. What this site actually does today

As of the date at the top of this page, futureofinteriorspaces.com is a largely static site. It uses standard web fonts (Google Fonts) and an animation library (GSAP) to render its typography, layout, and movement effects, and browser-native, on-page effects such as a custom cursor and scroll-triggered animations — none of which involve setting cookies. The Site also includes a newsletter sign-up form and a contact link; neither currently sets any cookies.

4. Third-party resources loaded by this Site

Although this Site does not set cookies, a small number of resources are loaded from third-party servers as a technical part of displaying the page — specifically, web fonts from Google Fonts and the GSAP animation library from the Cloudflare cdnjs library. Loading a resource from a third-party server necessarily sends that server some basic technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, as part of the request — this is how any third-party-hosted resource works, and is separate from cookie-based tracking. We have chosen widely used, reputable providers for these resources and do not use them to build a profile of your activity.

5. If this changes

If we later introduce functionality that does use cookies — for example, a live newsletter platform or an analytics tool — we will update this Cookie Policy first, describe clearly what is being set and why, and, where required by law, ask for your consent before doing so.

6. Controlling cookies in your browser

Even though this Site does not currently set cookies, most browsers let you view, manage, and delete cookies set by any website through their settings (usually found under "Privacy" or "Site settings"). You can also configure your browser to block cookies by default, though doing so may affect how some other websites function.

7. Contact

Questions about this Cookie Policy can be sent to hello@futureofinteriorspaces.com.

UAE & the Gulf Design Economy

A premium-first market where renovation outpaces new build, Dubai and Abu Dhabi split the country's design spend, and a record regional hotel pipeline keeps hospitality interiors on a multi-year build cycle.

Overview

A market led by renovation and luxury

The UAE's interior design market is estimated at roughly USD 1.77 billion in 2025, rising to about USD 1.94 billion in 2026 and projected to reach USD 3.11 billion by 2031 — a compound annual growth rate near 9.83%, according to Mordor Intelligence. Unusually for a fast-growing market, growth here is not primarily a new-build story: renovation and remodelling already account for over two-thirds of market value (68.05%), and the premium and luxury price tier is both the largest segment (48.72% share) and the fastest-growing one, expanding at over 10% a year.

Demand is geographically concentrated. Dubai alone represents just over half of the national market (51.74% share, growing at 7.18% CAGR), with Abu Dhabi contributing roughly a third (33.30% share). The residential segment leads at 56.72% of the market, but commercial interiors — hospitality, retail, corporate — are the segment institutional investors are watching most closely, underpinned by a regional hotel construction pipeline that hit an all-time high in 2026.

Sector Breakdown

Where UAE design spend concentrates

Residential
56.72% of 2025 market value. Villa and apartment renovation for both resident and second-home/investor owners across Dubai and Abu Dhabi.
Hospitality
UAE hotel construction pipeline: 103 projects / c. 24,985 rooms as of Q2 2026, the region's third-largest after Saudi Arabia.
Commercial & Retail
Growing at 6.86% CAGR to 2031, driven by corporate relocations, free-zone expansion and mixed-use retail development.
Renovation & Remodelling
68.05% of 2025 market value — the dominant service type, growing at 7.92% CAGR as older stock across both emirates is upgraded.
Premium & Luxury
48.72% share and the fastest-growing price tier at 10.12% CAGR, reflecting the UAE's positioning as a global luxury real estate destination.
New-Build Fit-Out
The smaller share of total value but tied directly to greenfield hospitality, residential towers and giga-project delivery across both emirates.

Overview

A Mature Market Under Renewed Pressure

The United Kingdom remains one of the most established interior design markets FOIS Intelligence tracks, anchored by London's position as a global hub for high-end residential, hospitality and workplace design. Growth is increasingly coming from outside the capital, however, as Manchester, Edinburgh and Bristol build design-led commercial and mixed-use pipelines of their own.

Two forces are shaping specification decisions in the UK more than anywhere else FOIS Intelligence covers: tightening embodied-carbon regulation in commercial retrofit, and a hybrid-work-driven reallocation of office floor plates toward hospitality-grade amenity space.
